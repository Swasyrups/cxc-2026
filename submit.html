CxC 2026

Competition
Rulebook

The complete rules and guidelines for CxC 2026. All registered participants are expected to read and agree to these terms before competing.

Last updated: June 2026 Version 1.0
In this document
1. Eligibility 2. Format 3. Recipe Rules 4. Swa Requirement 5. Video Submission 6. Scoring 7. Conduct
Section 01

Eligibility

01

Open to all practising baristas and bartenders across India. No minimum experience required.

02

Stage 1 is an individual competition. You register and submit as a solo entrant — baristas and bartenders compete in separate tracks.

03

Shortlisted individuals from Stage 1 will be paired by the CxC organisers into barista–bartender teams for the regionals.

04

Employees of Raw Earth Foods & Beverages Pvt. Ltd. and their immediate family members are not eligible.

Section 02

Competition Format

01

Stage 1 — Individual online submission. Video + recipe card. Judged remotely by the CxC panel.

02

Stage 2 — Regionals in Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi. Shortlisted teams compete live. Top 3 teams per city advance.

03

Stage 3 — Grand Finale on 8 September 2026, Bangalore. 15-minute live showcase per team. Live judging panel.

Section 03

Recipe Rules

03

You can submit a mocktail, cocktail or a coffee beverage recipe.

01

One drink submission per person. The recipe must be original and created by the participant specifically for CxC 2026.

01

If at any stage of the competition, it is found that the recipe has been copied from somewhere, the individual and team will both be disqualified.

02

The drink must not have been publicly served at any commercial venue in the 12 months preceding the submission date.

03

All ingredients must be legally purchasable in India. No prohibited substances.

04

Submission deadline is 25 July 2026, 11:59 PM IST. No extensions will be granted under any circumstances.

Section 04

Swa Syrup Requirement

01

At least one Swa Artisanal Syrup must feature as a named ingredient in the final drink. Entries without Swa will be disqualified.

01

Minimum of 20ml of any one syrup must be used.

02

You may use multiple Swa syrups. There is no upper limit.

02

You can only use the Swa syrup flavours sent to you in the sample kit.

03

The Swa syrup sample kit dispatched on registration must be used in your video demonstration.

Section 05

Video Submission

01

Every recipe submission needs to be accompanied with a recipe card.

02

You need to tell us the exact ingredients, tools, techniques and garnish used.

03

You will need to submit a finished photo of the drink as well.

Section 06

Video Submission

01

Submit a video between 60 and 90 seconds showing your drink preparation from start to finish.

02

The video must show your face and hands. Voice narration explaining your drink concept is strongly encouraged.

03

Accepted formats: MP4, MOV. Maximum file size: 250MB. Minimum resolution: 720p.

04

No post-production effects, AI-generated visuals, or footage from commercial shoots. Raw, authentic footage only.

Section 07

Scoring

Stage 1 entries are scored out of 100 points across five criteria.

Craft & Technique25

Precision in preparation, consistency, and mastery of your craft — shaking, stirring, brewing, or building.

Flavour & Balance25

Does the drink taste great? Are sweetness, acidity, bitterness, and body in harmony?

Swa Integration20

How thoughtfully is the Swa syrup used? Does it elevate the drink or feel like an afterthought?

Creativity & Narrative20

Is this drink original and memorable? Does it tell a story — about a place, a memory, a culture?

Presentation10

Visual appeal of the finished drink and how it's served — glassware, garnish, overall finish.

Section 08

Conduct & Rights

01

Judges' decisions are final and binding. No appeals will be entertained.

02

Any attempt to contact judges directly about your submission will result in immediate disqualification.

03

CxC organisers reserve the right to disqualify any entry that does not meet the spirit of the competition.

04

By submitting, you grant Swa Artisanal Syrups and CxC the non-exclusive right to use your name, photo, video, and recipe content for promotional purposes including social media, paid advertising, and the Swa recipe bank — with full credit given to you as the creator.

04

Swa will not be held responsible for misconduct or plagiarism by the participants.

04

If the participant fails to meet the deadlines or complete requirements, the organisers reserve the right to disqualify the candidate.
