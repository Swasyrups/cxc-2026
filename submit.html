CxC 2026 — Regional Round

Regional Round
Rules

Rules for competing teams. Read carefully before the event.

Last updated: August 2026 Version 2.0
In this document
1. Team 2. Format 3. The Drink 4. Swa Requirement 5. Submission 6. On the Day 7. Scoring 8. Conduct
Section 01

Team & Eligibility

01

One barista + one bartender per team. Both must be physically present on the day.

02

Both partners must contribute to the recipe and submission.

03

Employees of Raw Earth Foods & Beverages Pvt. Ltd. and their immediate family are not eligible.

Section 02

Competition Format

01

8 minutes to prep, present your drink, and tell your story.

02

2 minutes for questions from the judges.

03

2 minutes to clean up your station.

04

Make 5 servings — 4 for judges, 1 for photography.

05

Top 2 teams per regional advance to the Grand Finale, Bangalore, 8 September 2026.

Section 03

The Drink

01

The drink must contain coffee and at least 10ml of Swa Artisanal Syrup.

02

Alcohol is optional. If used, follow venue rules.

03

Pre-prepped ingredients are allowed. Garnishes may be prepared in advance. All must be declared in the submission.

04

Homemade syrups, infusions, bitters, and fat washes are permitted. The recipe for all homemade components must be declared in the submission — organisers may test these before use.

05

The recipe on the day must exactly match the submitted recipe. Changes may result in point deductions or disqualification.

06

The recipe must be original, not publicly served at any venue in the 12 months prior to the event.

Section 04

Swa Syrup Requirement

01

Minimum 10ml of at least one Swa syrup must be used. Entries without Swa will be disqualified.

02

You may use multiple Swa syrups — no upper limit.

03

Only Swa syrups provided at the venue may be used.

Section 05

Submission

01

Submit online by 11 August 2026. Both partners can log in and edit the shared submission.

02

Mandatory: a video of the drink being made, a finished drink photo, an ingredients & setup photo, and a full written recipe with tools and process.

03

All pre-prepped components must be declared with their full recipes.

04

Declare glassware requirements and any special venue needs. Not guaranteed — we will confirm what's possible.

05

Once submitted, the entry is locked. Do not change your recipe, tools, or process after submission.

06

Print and carry your recipe card, ingredient list, and homemade component recipes on the day.

Section 06

On the Day

01

Report to venue by 8:30 AM. Any team not present at the start of their slot may be disqualified.

02

Carry all your own tools and ingredients including pre-prepped components (sealed, labelled).

03

Available at venue: Blue Tokai coffee (3 roasts), Swa Artisanal Syrups, basic ice cubes.

04

Not available: alcohol, specialty ice, any other ingredients. Bring everything else yourself.

05

Glassware declared in submission can be used. If not declared, carry your own.

06

Tag @swa.artisanalsyrups on all posts from the event.

Section 07

Scoring

Scored out of 100 points.

Taste & Balance30

Sweetness, acidity, bitterness, coffee, and body in harmony.

Story & Presentation25

Compelling narrative, confident presentation from both partners.

Technique & Execution20

Clean workstation, precise technique, efficient within 8 minutes.

Originality & Collaboration15

Original concept, genuine collaboration on build and story.

Hero Ingredients10

How thoughtfully Swa and coffee elevate the drink.

Section 08

Conduct & Rights

01

Judges' decisions are final and binding. No appeals.

02

Plagiarism or unsportsmanlike conduct results in disqualification of both team members.

03

CxC organisers reserve the right to disqualify any team not meeting the spirit of the competition.

04

By competing, you grant Swa and CxC the right to use your name, photo, video, and recipe for promotional purposes — with full credit.

05

Dates, venues, format, and prizes are subject to change without prior notice.

Rules

Rules for the 9 finalist teams. Read carefully before 6 September.

Section 01

About the Finale

There are 2 rounds — one you prepare for from now to 6 September, and one you'll face live on the day of the Finale. Same team, same chemistry — new challenges.

CxC ClassAct — the main round. A classic cocktail, caffeinated. Tests preparation, precision, and craft.

CxC Dark Secret — the surprise-on-the-day round. Work with a secret ingredient and a limited pantry to craft a recipe in 30 minutes. Tests instinct, real-world bar thinking, and how you handle a curveball.

01

Team size: 2 — one barista + one bartender. Same team as in Regionals.

02

Both partners must contribute to the recipe and the submission.

03

Both must be physically present on the day. Absence of a team member disqualifies the team.

04

The submission video must show both of you making the drink together.

05

Every drink must include coffee (brewed live), Swa syrup, and alcohol — mandatorily.

06

Judged by 3 Tasting Judges + 2 Technical Judges (one for coffee, one for spirit).

Section 02

CxC ClassAct

The Theme: Make a classic cocktail, desi and caffeinated.

What we're testing

01

Fundamental soundness — can you build a technically sound drink.

02

Ability to work with flavour — can you make an Indian ingredient belong in a Western classic.

03

Risk-taking — are you playing safe, or actually pushing the twist.

How it works

01

Each team chooses a Classic Cocktail from a given list.

02

You get 10 days to think through your creative twist.

03

Submit the recipe online by 6 September. This is the recipe you will present on 8 September — no changes.

04

Present the recipe live in Bangalore on 8 September.

05

Use any of the 5 Swa syrups, chosen by you, for the cocktail. You need a minimum amount of Swa syrup in it.

Online Submission

01

Log in at cxc.drinkswa.com with your registered email — OTP login.

02

Both partners can log in and edit the submission.

03

Submit: 1 video of you making the drink (together, if possible), 1 photo of the finished drink, and a written breakdown of your process, tools & ingredients — including anything pre-prepared.

04

Once submitted, your entry is locked — same recipe, same tools, same process, on the Finale.

05

Submission deadline: 6 September 2026.

On Presentation Day

01

Each team gets a total assigned slot of 15 minutes — 3 min Prep (can be used to brew coffee), 8 min Make + Present + Story, 2 min Serving + Judges' Questions, 2 min Clean-up.

02

Make 4 pours — 3 for the taste judges, 1 for photography.

03

Your story: why this spin, for this specific drink — not "why this assigned drink."

Section 03

CxC Dark Secret

The Theme: Surprise ingredient round. On-the-spot recipe making.

What we're testing

01

Team chemistry.

02

Thinking on your feet.

03

AI-free creativity.

04

Working within constraints.

How it works

01

A list of 9 secret ingredients will be compiled — one per team.

02

Draw of lots decides your presentation order for this round.

03

In that same sequence, each team picks a rival team — whoever you think is your toughest competition — and hands them the toughest ingredient left on the list.

04

If you get a disadvantage from an early presentation slot, you also get the advantage of picking the toughest ingredient for the toughest opponent.

05

Draw of lots and ingredient selection will be done before 10am.

06

When the round begins, every team gets 30 minutes to think, with access to a basic pantry.

07

At the end of the 30 minutes, hand over a handwritten recipe to the judges — no changes after, while other teams present. It must contain the ingredients you chose from the pantry, recipe method, glassware, and garnish details.

08

Each team gets a total assigned slot of 15 minutes to make & present, in the draw-of-lots sequence — 3 min prep, 8 min make, 2 min judge serving + Q&A, 2 min clean-up.

09

Storytelling — walk the judges through your thought process, live.

Section 05

Things to Note

01

Pre-prepped elements (garnish, infusions, syrups) are allowed — only if declared in your submission.

02

Technical Timeout is allowed for a genuine equipment malfunction. Raise your hand, your clock pauses, our standby technician steps in. Once the issue is fixed, the clock resumes. Not valid for unfamiliarity with equipment.

Disqualification criteria

01

Both team members not being present at the assigned time.

02

Brewing coffee before your total assigned time slot begins.

03

Coaching or outside assistance during Prep or Make time.

04

Exceeding your total assigned time slot by 2 minutes.

Section 06

Scoring

Two rounds, weighted: CxC ClassAct — 70% · CxC Dark Secret — 30%

Within each round, two judging pools: Taste — 70% (3 Taste Judges) · Technical — 30% (1 Coffee Technical Judge + 1 Spirit Technical Judge)

Technical judges observe your process live — they do not taste the drink before scoring.

·

Negative scoring: −2 points for every 15 seconds (or part of it) over your total assigned time window (10 min for CxC Dark Secret, 15 min for CxC ClassAct).
